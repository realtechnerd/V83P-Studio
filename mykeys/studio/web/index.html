Good to know

  • Editing needs the cable with the switch on wired. Saved settings work on 2.4G and Bluetooth.
  • Fn + 1 / 2 / 3 pick Bluetooth hosts (hold 3 s to pair), Fn + 4 2.4G, Fn + 5 USB.
  • Fn + Esc for 3 seconds resets all settings to default.
  • Firmware: install, update or go back to the original from the Firmware card. Updates keep your settings.
